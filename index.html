AI in TVET and Employment Promotion – Applying AI Sensibly

Virtual impulse keynote (12–15 min) by Hannes Tegelbeckers. Three pillars – labour market data, training for AI-supported systems, training with AI – plus an interlude on how prompting changes the way we work with computers, and why the AI itself never touches the computer.

How to view: The interactive web presentation runs directly in the browser (arrow keys or presenter clicker, N for speaker notes, F for full screen) and contains the simulations. The same keynote is also available as a LiaScript course with narration and quizzes – no login, no installation. Below you find the three example LiaScript presentations the keynote was built from.
